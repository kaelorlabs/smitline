# Hold listener test audio

G.711 mu-law WAV files, 8 kHz mono: the same bytes phone_line feeds `HoldListener`, with a WAV
header so any player opens them (and git treats them as binary). Cut from the business side of
Smitline's own phone simulation of 2026-10-22 (scenario `menu-hold-rep`), whose menu, hold
music, announcement and rep were all generated by our simulator.

| File | Seconds | What |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `hold_music.wav` | 12.0 | Hold music (plucked chords) only. |
| `announcement.wav` | 9.6 | Hold music, then the music pauses for the recorded "Your call is important to us. Please stay on the line, and the next available representative will be with you shortly." (1.54-9.13 s), then music again. |
| `announcement_again.wav` | 9.65 | The same announcement (1.64-9.23 s), this time over another stretch of the hold music 10 dB down, with other music before and after it (joined with 40 ms crossfades). |
| `rep_greeting.wav` | 6.8 | The end of the hold music (until 1.34 s), a silent line, then the rep: "Thanks for holding, this is Dana in billing. Who am I speaking with?" Her voice starts at 3.26 s. |
